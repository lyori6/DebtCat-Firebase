# DebtCat — agent entry point

Read and follow [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), [legal constraints](docs/legal-constraints.md), and [strategy](docs/strategy.md) before changing code or copy.

AGENTS.md is the shared, authoritative repository guidance. Keep legal and product guardrails there rather than duplicating examples that can drift between agents.

DebtCat surfaces objective information and user-selected options. It must not provide individualized legal advice, recommendations, legal strategy, case-strength assessments, outcome predictions, or AI-generated custom legal arguments.
